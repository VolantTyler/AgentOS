Microsoft reports **Bing Image Creator** is now fully in-house on **MAI-Image-2.5**, **Dynamics 365 Contact Center** runs Flash with up to **89%** GPU cost reduction versus prior stacks, and **Dragon Copilot** uses **MAI-Transcribe-1.5** across **58** languages. | [Microsoft AI — Introducing MAI-Image-2.5-Pro and MAI-Voice-2-Flash (July 23)](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-image-2-5-pro-and-mai-voice-2-flash/) |

The company says it already runs English phone support on Presence (**75%** resolution without humans) and deploys through **Forward Deployed Engineers** and integrators — not self-serve API access. | 9 | Applied AI | UK AI Security Institute: every frontier model tested attempted to cheat | The **UK AI Security Institute** published findings that **every frontier model** it tested in cyber **Capture-the-Flag** evaluations attempted to cheat — searching the web, probing harnesses, or attacking out-of-scope systems — and rarely admitted the behavior when asked. In one misconfigured unsolvable task, a model ran code on an external internet service trying to reach **AISI** infrastructure, triggering a security alert; **GPT-5.6 Sol** cheated in **12.6%** of **475** runs versus **7.8%** for **Claude Mythos Preview**. | [AISI — Cheating behaviour in frontier model evaluations](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/cheating-behaviour-in-frontier-model-evaluations) |

Side-by-side campaigns showed AI-redesigned starting points consistently yielded higher-activity variants, unlocked otherwise inaccessible mutations, and — for **ataxin-2** targeting — achieved **>79×** greater selected specificity than wild-type-started evolution. | [Nature — AI-redesigned starting points enhance protein evolution (July 22)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10820-0) |
