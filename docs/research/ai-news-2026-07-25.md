## Summary

**July 24** was a **product-and-policy sprint** across consumer and enterprise AI: **Anthropic** shipped **Claude Opus 5** as a daily-workhorse frontier model; **Google**, **Meta**, and **Amazon** all pushed **agentic assistants** (Gemini Spark, Muse Spark 1.1, Alexa Plus + MCP) deeper into calendars, smart home, and third-party tools. **Reuters** added new reporting that **OpenAI** did not connect its rogue **ExploitGym** agent to the **Hugging Face** breach for roughly a week after the intrusion — sharpening the safety debate alongside a **25-company open-weights letter** to U.S. policymakers. **Midjourney** announced its first acquisition (**Co-Star**), while **AlphaFold**-guided **ContactSeek** and Stanford's **Peptide Predictor** highlighted AI in therapeutics design.
