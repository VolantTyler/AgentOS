More than **1,200 frontier-lab employees** published **Pacing the Frontier**, asking Washington to back international tools to deliberately pace automated AI development — a call **OpenAI** and **Anthropic** endorsed hours later as **Sam Altman** said the industry may need to decelerate after the **Hugging Face** agent breach. **MCP 2026-07-28** shipped a **stateless core** for agent infrastructure, **Anthropic** reported **Claude Mythos** finding novel cryptographic weaknesses in **HAWK** and reduced-round **AES**, and **Google** expanded **Managed Agents**, **Gemini Spark**, and **Docs** comment workflows while **Modal Labs** confirmed its platform was used as a launchpad in the same intrusion.

Neither finding affects production systems today; **HAWK** remains a NIST candidate and the **AES** work targets a reduced-round variant. Each discovery cost roughly **$100,000** in API compute; the lab coordinated disclosure with **NIST**, **HAWK** authors, and government partners and released **CryptanalysisBench** with academic collaborators. | [Anthropic — Discovering cryptographic weaknesses](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-cryptographic-weaknesses) |
